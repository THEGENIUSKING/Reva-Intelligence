## Brand & Style
The brand personality reflects institutional authority, catalytic energy, and rigorous investment acumen tailored to the emerging market frontier. Designed for an elite venture studio, the visual narrative combines the grounding discipline of high finance with the velocity of tech innovation.

The aesthetic is Modern Tactile Minimalism: warm, ash-infused tonal backgrounds evoke organic stability, while vivid solar orange accents inject urgency, conviction, and forward momentum. The interface rejects generic cold tech palettes in favor of refined earth-ash neutrals, generous negative space, crisp micro-typography, and bespoke mathematical structures. The emotional impression is analytical, sharp, and decisively modern.

## Colors
The color hierarchy is strictly curated around focused signals and contextual utility:

- **Primary Action (`#E07000`)**: A saturated, decisive orange reserved for high-conviction interaction targets, primary actions, and key directional trends. Hover state shifts to `#C96400`; active/pressed to `#B35800`.
- **Primary Tint Accents**: Light orange tints provide structural feedback without visual noise. `#FFF4E8` is applied to row-level active highlights, badge grounds, and focus halos. `#FFE2C4` serves as selected container borders, metric card overlays, and subtle indicator fills.
- **Charcoal Text & Surface (`#2B2B2B`)**: Anchor tone for headings, critical numeric reads, primary body copy, and dense dark interface surfaces (such as side rail utilities or contrast banners).
- **Ash Greys (Tonal Scale)**:
  - Canvas Base: `#F7F7F6` (warm chalk neutral).
  - Raised Surface / Cards: `#FFFFFF` and `#EFEFED`.
  - Borders & Hairlines: `#E2E2DF`.
  - Secondary Copy / Icons: `#8A8A86`.
  - Deep Tertiary / Metadata: `#5C5C58`.
- **System States**: Semantic cues are reserved strictly for binary operational health. Green (`#1E824C` / background `#EAF5EE`) is applied exclusively to validated, outperforming, or healthy stage gates. Red (`#D32F2F` / background `#FDE8E8`) signifies critical blockers, write-downs, or invalid hypotheses.

## Typography
Typography is anchored on Work Sans, pairing human proportions with crisp, geometric discipline. Metrics and institutional reporting demand sharp numerical readability and unambiguous optical alignment.

- **Scale & Hierarchy**: Headlines use confident medium and bold weights with subtle negative tracking to balance visual weight against generous white and ash space. Body copy remains legible and unadorned at 14px and 16px with neutral line heights for rapid dashboard scanning.
- **Micro Uppercase Accents**: Section overlines, table headers, column categorizations, and investment thesis tags leverage `label-caps` set in full uppercase with tracking (`0.08em`) to enforce structural authority without adding physical bulk.
- **Tabular Numerics**: Financial metrics, ticket sizes, valuation runs, and KPIs must render with tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to preserve vertical columnar alignment across financial data grids.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout adheres to a flexible 12-column grid predicated on deliberate density zones. Venture workflows demand low-friction side-by-side comparisons (market validation vs. financial models) alongside expansive canvas areas for thesis breakdown.

- **Breakpoints & Grids**:
  - **Desktop (1280px+)**: 12-column fluid grid, `2rem` outer margins, `1.5rem` gutters. Accommodates standard fixed 260px collapsible intelligence navigation rail with fluid content staging.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1279px)**: 8-column layout, `1.5rem` outer margins, `1rem` gutters. Sidebars fold into an off-canvas drawer or top horizontal secondary navigation.
  - **Mobile (<768px)**: 4-column layout, `1rem` outer margins, `0.75rem` gutters. Metric cards stack vertically; multi-column analysis grids translate to scrollable tabs.
- **Spacing Rhythm**: Spacing operates on a strict base-4/base-8 vertical scale. Component padding relies on `space-md` (16px) and `space-lg` (24px) to retain breathing room within modular card surfaces. Dense internal data rows scale down to `space-sm` (8px).

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is produced through subtle tonal separation and directional glow rather than heavy dropshadows, ensuring interfaces remain crisp and light.

- **Tonal Layering**: The primary foundation rests on canvas ash (`#F7F7F6`). Surface components (cards, decks, sidebars) leverage pure white (`#FFFFFF`) or light surface ash (`#EFEFED`) delineated by a fine 1px border (`#E2E2DF`).
- **Orange Radiance (Diffuse Atmosphere)**: Key feature spotlights, executive summary callouts, and active focal points use an ambient orange radial glow:
  - `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -8px rgba(224, 112, 0, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(224, 112, 0, 0.04);`
- **Ambient Neutral Elevation**:
  - Level 1 (Resting Cards/Modules): Flat surface with border `1px solid #E2E2DF`.
  - Level 2 (Hover/Interacted Cards): `box-shadow: 0 8px 24px -4px rgba(43, 43, 43, 0.06);` with border transition to `#D6D6D2`.
  - Level 3 (Modals, Overlays, Command Palette): `box-shadow: 0 20px 48px -12px rgba(43, 43, 43, 0.16);` coupled with an ultra-soft `#2B2B2B` backdrop scrim at 40% opacity.

## Shapes
Shapes strike a balance between executive precision and warm, modern tactile appeal.

- **Surfaces & Cards**: Base cards, charts, and intelligence panels adhere to rounded radii of `1rem` (16px) or `1.125rem` (18px) for larger parent containers, eliminating visual aggression while maintaining containment.
- **Interactive Controls**: Inputs, standard buttons, dropdown menus, and segmented control containers use `0.5rem` (8px) to provide structural distinction against parent card shells.
- **Pills & Chips**: Status badges, lifecycle stage identifiers, round tags, and pill toggles are explicitly full-rounded (`9999px`), giving them a crisp, tactile token aesthetic that separates metadata from structural inputs.

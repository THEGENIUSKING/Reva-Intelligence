Co-op Anchor Aggregators

Do not onboard farmers individually. Execute master service level agreements with established state farmer aggregators like AFEX hubs or regional off-takers with 3-year performance audits.

Never approve venture Capex for systems that lack at least 14 hours of autonomous cold retention via phase change materials (PCM) or gel ice thermal storage due to national transmission frequency collapses.

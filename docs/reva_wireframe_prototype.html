verified_user What to Apply When Deploying in Nigeria
  • check_circle
    Anchor on Off-Takers, Not Subsistence Farmers: Sign anchor take-or-pay cooling capacity agreements with organized FMCG processors (e.g. Tomato Jos, GB Foods) rather than relying solely on individual farmer micro-payments.
  • check_circle
    Deploy Thermal Energy (Ice Bank) Storage: Emulate Ecozen India by utilizing phase-change water/ice thermal storage to achieve 36 hours of cold retention, eliminating expensive lithium battery replacement costs under Nigerian heat.
  • check_circle
    Offline-First IoT & Agent Verification: Integrate USSD and Bluetooth token activation for cold room doors so operations continue during frequent rural GSM network dropouts.
gpp_bad What to Avoid in Nigeria (Costly Pitfalls)
  • cancel
    Avoid Pure Direct Equipment Sales: Smallholders cannot afford ₦5M–₦15M cold unit capital outlays. Avoid outright asset sales without asset-finance partners or shared infrastructure models.
  • cancel
    Avoid Pure Card-Based Recurring Subscriptions: Card mandates fail at over 40% in Nigeria. Implement automated wallet debits, USSD push notifications, or physical market agent collection.
  • cancel
    Avoid Unhedged Foreign Equipment Financing: Equipment imported with dollar-denominated loans will trigger debt distress when Naira depreciates. Source local assembly or hedge via export-linked produce.
